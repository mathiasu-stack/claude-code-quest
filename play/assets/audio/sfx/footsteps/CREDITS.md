  https://freesound.org/people/ceberation/sounds/235524/
- **Eelke** — `boots on aluminum ladder 01` (the `metal` set)
  https://freesound.org/people/Eelke/sounds/462598/

## What we changed
Re-encoded to mono 44.1 kHz MP3 and kept 5 variations per surface. The game has
no carpet recording in the source pack, so the `carpet/` set is the concrete
("boots") samples low-pass-filtered (~560 Hz) to read as a soft, muffled tread.

This attribution is surfaced in-game on the Credits screen (see app.js / the
in-game credits) to satisfy the CC-BY attribution requirement.
